# Al Ghurair Procurement Genie Demo

## Goal

Provide a reusable, self-contained procurement analytics demonstration for
UAE construction bid evaluation.

## Experience

Procurement users receive six focused Genie spaces covering bids, suppliers,
financials, market intelligence, contract performance, and risk. The spaces
share a realistic synthetic dataset and a single SQL warehouse.

## Components

- Six Databricks Genie spaces
- Sixteen synthetic Unity Catalog Delta tables
- Serverless data setup job
- Databricks Asset Bundle for lifecycle management
- One-command installer and operating documentation

## Demo storyline

1. Review the Deira Waterfront RFP and rank submitted bids.
2. Compare bid rates with UAE construction benchmarks.
3. Inspect supplier ratings and certifications.
4. Assess financial, compliance, and delivery risk.
5. Compare historical contract and SLA performance.
6. Benchmark market competition and construction cost indices.

## Data policy

The dataset is synthetic and is suitable for demonstrations, not production
decision-making.
